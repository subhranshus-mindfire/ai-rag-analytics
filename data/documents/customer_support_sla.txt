# Acme Corporation - Technical Support & Service Level Agreement (SLA)

## 1. Support Tiers & Coverage Hours
- Consumer Tier: Standard email support available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST. Guaranteed initial response within 48 business hours.
- SMB Tier: Priority ticketing and email support available Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST. Guaranteed initial response within 12 business hours.
- Enterprise Tier: 24/7/365 dedicated telephone and live chat support with a dedicated Technical Account Manager (TAM). Guaranteed critical incident response within 1 hour.

## 2. Service Availability & Uptime Guarantee
Acme guarantees 99.99% monthly service uptime across all Cloud Vector Engine and API endpoints. 

## 3. Downtime Service Credits
In the event uptime falls below the 99.99% threshold during a calendar month:
- 99.0% to 99.9%: 10% monthly service credit.
- 95.0% to 98.9%: 25% monthly service credit.
- Below 95.0%: 50% monthly service credit.
Service credits must be requested within 30 days of the affected calendar month.
