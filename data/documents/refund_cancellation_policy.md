# Acme Corporation - Subscription Refund & Cancellation Policy

## 1. Overview
This policy outlines customer entitlements and procedures regarding subscription cancellations, disputes, and financial refunds for all software and cloud analytics products.

## 2. Annual Subscription Refunds
Annual software subscriptions may be refunded in full within 30 days of the original purchase date upon written notice to billing@acme.example.com. Refund requests submitted after the 30-day window are ineligible for refunds, but access will remain active through the end of the paid term.

## 3. Monthly Subscription Cancellations
Monthly subscriptions may be cancelled at any time through the customer billing portal. Cancellations take effect at the conclusion of the current monthly billing cycle. Partial month refunds or pro-rated credits are not issued for mid-cycle cancellations.

## 4. Hardware and Physical Security Keys
Hardware returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery provided the item is unopened and in its original tamper-evident packaging. A standard 15% restocking fee applies to all physical equipment returns.

## 5. Refund Processing Timeline
Approved refunds are credited to the original method of payment within 5 to 7 business days following financial audit authorization.
