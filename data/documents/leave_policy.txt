# Acme Corporation - Comprehensive Employee Leave Policy

## 1. Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per calendar year, accrued at 1.67 days per month. A maximum of 5 unused PTO days can be rolled over into the following calendar year; any remaining excess unused days expire on December 31st.

## 2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave annually for personal illness or family medical care. For absences exceeding three consecutive working days, medical documentation from a licensed healthcare provider is required.

## 3. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Secondary caregivers are eligible for 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave. All parental leave must be taken within the first 12 months following the child's arrival.

## 4. Bereavement Leave
Employees receive up to 5 consecutive paid days off for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling), and 3 paid days for extended family members.

## 5. Request & Approval Procedure
All planned leave requests must be submitted through the HR Portal at least two weeks in advance. Emergency leaves must be reported to the direct manager within 2 hours of normal business start time.
